## 3. How to read these numbers

- **Synthetic, deterministic, single machine.** The benchmark ran on one Windows 11 laptop (Ryzen 7 5700U) in one Python process with SQLite. The results show the logic is correct and reproducible. They are **not** production throughput or reliability figures.
- **The baseline is deliberately weak** (it checks the role only). It is a lower bound, not a model of existing UAE government IAM. Case-bound configuration B is the fairer comparison.
- **Labels come from request categories,** never from any configuration's decision. Seed `20260`; the dataset SHA-256 is recorded in `manifest.json`.
- **Prevention is not the same as detection.** Monitoring rules flagged 657 / 720 unauthorized requests. Expired-mandate requests, for example, are prevented by authorization but have no dedicated monitoring rule.
- **Recovery fix.** The 2/20 result was a real weakness: agent-wide counters attached incidents to unrelated cases. It was diagnosed and fixed, and the historical result is kept and shown.

## 4. What is not evidenced

- **No live AI model was tested.** The demo uses a labelled, deterministic offline planner. Nothing is claimed about how a real model behaves with injected text.
- **No integration with any government system.** No real payments or real citizen records.
- **No production deployment, high availability or external audit anchoring.**
- Behaviour Watch thresholds are hand-set rules, not AI threat prediction.
