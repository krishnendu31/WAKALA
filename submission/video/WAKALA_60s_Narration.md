## Why it is worded this way

- **"Deliberately triggered test"**: the cross-citizen request is a scripted synthetic scenario. No AI model chose to attack.
- **"Blocks it before any protected data is returned"**: the gateway decision was *denied*, and denied requests are never dispatched to the protected service.
- **"Behaviour Watch raises an incident"**: the two mechanisms are kept separate. The *gateway* denies the request; Behaviour Watch then records the CROSS_CITIZEN_ACCESS signal and opens the incident, which is visible on screen.
- **"Document Shield quarantines hidden document instructions"** names what it detected here. The narration does not say it catches every prompt injection.
- **"Synthetic benchmarks"** and **"tests policies safely"**: no production or reliability claim is made.
