# WAKALA — The Moment Before · asset prompts for an external video generator

**Why this file exists:** this session had **no text-to-video generator**, so the delivered film uses original local motion graphics. If the team has access to a licensed generator, these prompts would produce photoreal or 3D plates to replace or underlay the conceptual scenes. The real-prototype scenes (0:32–0:40 and 0:49–0:55) must **not** be replaced: they are the proof.

**Where to put the clips:** `submission/video/cinematic_assets/`, one MP4 per shot, named as below (1920×1080 or larger, 24–30 fps, no text or logos, no watermark). Check the generator's licence allows commercial and competition use, and keep the licence record with the clips.

**Global style for every prompt:** *premium technology launch film, realistic lighting, anamorphic look, shallow depth of field, slow deliberate camera, deep sapphire navy and midnight blue palette with warm UAE gold and emerald teal accents, soft pearl highlights, restrained, elegant, no text, no logos, no people's faces in close-up, no hackers in hoodies, no flying code, no circuit boards, no holographic UI panels.*

**Negative prompt:** *text, letters, watermark, logo, user interface, dashboard, code, matrix rain, circuit board, hoodie, mask, skull, padlock icon, glitch, cartoon, oversaturated neon, lens-flare overload.*

To use generated plates, either composite them underneath those scenes (keeping the WAKALA graphics, the authorization boundary, the three checks and all honesty labels on top), or swap a scene's background for the matching clip during assembly. Any generated footage must keep the **CONCEPTUAL VISUALIZATION** tag. It must never show a fake WAKALA interface, a real government entity or invented security results.
